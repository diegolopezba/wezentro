Business product tours belong in the persistent app shell and persist versioned per-user step progress, because navigation must not unmount or restart the walkthrough.
Gate sales use a permanent public event URL but create separate anonymous payment sessions and single-use guestlist QR entries server-side; never reuse a bank QR or require an account at the gate.- Business tools (Crear options, Gestión tabs, Dashboard tabs) derive from `resolveBusinessModules` in src/lib/businessTypes.ts: category gives defaults, profile *_enabled flags add modules — keeps personalization in one place.
- Per-business Zentro fee terms live in the admin-only `business_fee_terms` table and are applied server-side via `loadFeeTerms` + `buildCharge` in every checkout function; buyers read them only through `get_checkout_fee_terms` — keeps business owners from editing their own commission.
- Multi-day events store per-day windows in events.event_days; full-pass entries are fanned out into one guestlist entry per day by DB triggers (assign_entry_event_day / issue_multi_day_pass_entries) so every checkout path issues day QRs without code changes, and check-in-guest enforces each entry's day window.
